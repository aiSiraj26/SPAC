## Key facts (sources and dates are in course.md)

- By mid-2024, 85% of about 300 companies that merged with SPACs through 2022 traded below $10, and 79% lost money from operations in 2023 (FTI Consulting).
- SPAC IPO investors earned 23.9% a year and sponsors more than 100% a year up to the merger, while investors who held the merged companies earned poor returns (Gahng, Ritter and Zhang, 2023).
- Of the de-SPACs closed in the 12 months to May 2026, 79% traded below $10 (Boardroom Alpha).
