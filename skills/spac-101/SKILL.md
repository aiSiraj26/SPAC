## Key facts (sources and dates are in course.md)

- A SPAC raises money in an IPO first, holds it in a trust, and has about 2 to 3 years to merge with a private company.
- Exchange rules require a deal within 36 months and a target worth at least 80% of the trust.
- The sponsor buys founder shares for about $25,000; this "promote" is usually about 20% of the shares.
- Shareholders can redeem their shares for about $10 plus interest instead of joining the merger.
- 144 SPAC IPOs raised $30.4 billion in 2025; 152 more raised about $29.7 billion in January–September 2026.
